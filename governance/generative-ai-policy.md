# Generative AI policy

This policy applies to pyOpenSci staff, volunteers, and community
contributors. It covers how you use AI tools in your work and in
communication across community spaces and events. This includes
documentation, lessons, the website, GitHub, Slack, Discord, social
media, and meetings.

If you are submitting a package to our software peer review program,
there are additional requirements in the
[generative AI policy in the software peer review guide](https://www.pyopensci.org/software-peer-review/our-process/generative-ai-policy.html).

## Principles

* Thoughtful use of AI tools is welcome when it helps you do your own work.
* AI can assist you, but you own what you share. Review every output,
  and make sure you understand it before you send or submit it.
* Be transparent. Disclose AI use where this policy asks you to.
* Protect our community. Do not create extra work for volunteers, and do
  not expose private information.

## How we use AI at pyOpenSci

### Meeting notes

We use Google Meet with Gemini to take notes in pyOpenSci meetings. We
find it efficient and effective for this purpose.

* The meeting host asks for consent before turning on note-taking.
* If anyone objects, we do not turn it on.
* Notes are shared with all attendees after the meeting.
* Attendees can ask for corrections or removals.
* No other AI note-takers or bots may join pyOpenSci meetings.

### Grammar and writing tools

Many of us use tools such as Grammarly to check grammar and clarity. We
welcome this. Always review suggestions and outputs before you send
anything.

### Translation and drafting

You can use AI tools for translation, grammar, and drafting. Review the
result before you share it. We prefer clear, honest language over
polished text that no one reviewed.

## What we do not allow

* **Automated agents.** Do not communicate in pyOpenSci spaces through
  automated agents or bots acting on your behalf.
* **Automated or bulk comments.** Do not send automated or bulk
  comments that others then have to sort through.
* **AI tools in our community Slack.** We have turned off all AI tools
  in our community Slack to protect our community.

## Communication in pyOpenSci spaces

Communication in pyOpenSci spaces must come from you. This includes
comments on GitHub, messages in Slack or Discord, and posts on social
media. AI can help you draft or polish your words, but you review them
and stand behind them.

## Contributions to pyOpenSci repositories

If you open a pull request or issue:

* Understand the change and be able to explain it.
* Review generated text and code before you submit it.
* Disclose your use of AI tools in the contribution.
* If a substantial part of the change was generated by an AI tool, say
  so in the pull request.

## Disclosure for other content

For documentation, lessons, blog posts, and other published content,
disclose when a substantial part was generated by an AI tool. Minor
use, such as grammar checks, does not need disclosure.

## Media content

This section covers images, video, audio, and other media.

* Disclose AI-generated or AI-altered media when you publish it.
* Do not use AI to generate or alter the likeness or voice of a real
  person without their consent.
* Review media for accuracy, bias, and licensing before you share it.

## Confidential information

Do not put private, confidential, or unpublished material into public
generative AI tools. This includes:

* Personal information about community members
* Unpublished peer review comments and submissions
* Member, funder, and financial data
* Private messages and unpublished drafts

## Software peer review

If you submit a package for pyOpenSci peer review, follow the separate
disclosure policy and disclose how you used generative AI tools. If you
are reviewing a package, you are not responsible for finding or fixing
machine-generated errors.

Read the
[generative AI policy in the software peer review guide](https://www.pyopensci.org/software-peer-review/our-process/generative-ai-policy.html)
before you submit or review a package.
